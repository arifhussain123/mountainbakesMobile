Design System · v2
Midnight & Saffron
Cool mist surfaces with deep midnight hero cards. A saffron gradient marks the one action that matters on each screen, with a soft glow so it reads at a glance.
Graphical buttons
Primary buttons are gradient pills with a glow and an arrow badge. Secondary buttons are white with a light shadow. The tab bar floats and shows the active tab as a lit-up chip.
One hand, one thumb
52px fields and buttons, 44px+ touch targets, primary actions pinned to the bottom. Forms open as bottom sheets so the list stays in view.
